About Our Hospital

Legacy of Healing & Modern Innovation

Wangdue Hospital (Bajo Hospital) has stood as a beacon of clinical safety and elite patient care. We provide a complete spectrum of diagnostic, therapeutic, and surgical treatments under one roof.

We believe in treating every individual with deep empathy, dignity, and cutting-edge operational intelligence to ensure the fastest possible recoveries.

Medical Departments

Cardiology

Comprehensive heart care utilizing minimally invasive therapeutics, modern diagnostics, and dedicated structural heart labs.

Neurology

Advanced treatment protocols for central and peripheral nervous system dynamics, stroke responses, and neuromuscular issues.

Pediatrics

Gentle and thorough child-centered healthcare parameters covering basic immunizations, growth reviews, and critical infant support.

Orthopedics

Surgical and therapeutic corrective care covering joint replacements, bone trauma architecture, and rehabilitative training.

General Surgery

Expert inpatient open surgeries and advanced, minimally invasive modern laparoscopic procedures for gastrointestinal conditions.

Optometry

Comprehensive visual health assessments, precise corrective lens formulations, and structured management plans for common vision anomalies.
